## G04C — Security Headers

### What was checked

Response headers on /, /admin/login, and /api/health for:
Strict-Transport-Security, Content-Security-Policy, X-Frame-Options,
X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy, X-XSS,
Set-Cookie.

### Results

| Header | / | /admin/login | /api/health |
|---|---|---|---|
| Set-Cookie secure | ✅ | ✅ | — |
| Set-Cookie httponly | ✅ (session) | ✅ (session) | — |
| SameSite=lax | ✅ | ✅ | — |
| Strict-Transport-Security | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Content-Security-Policy | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| X-Frame-Options | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| X-Content-Type-Options | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Referrer-Policy | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Permissions-Policy | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |

### Positive findings

- **Cookies** are `secure`, `httponly` (laravel_session), `samesite=lax` ✅
- This means session cookies cannot be sent over plain HTTP and are
  not readable from JavaScript.

### Findings — actionable security hardening

The following five headers are recommended and NOT currently present:

1. `Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains`
2. `Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'self'; ...`
3. `X-Frame-Options: DENY` (or `SAMEORIGIN`)
4. `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`
5. `Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`

**These can be added by a middleware without changing any controller
or route.** This will be recorded as a new actionable item.
