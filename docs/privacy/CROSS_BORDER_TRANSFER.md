# Cross-Border Data Transfer

**Legal Basis:** Proclamation 1321/2024, Art. 28-31
**Effective:** 2026-10-01

## 1. Principle
Data collected in Ethiopia is stored in Ethiopia where feasible. Transfers require lawful basis.

## 2. Current Transfers
| Service | Destination | Data | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Telegram | Russia / UAE | Auth, notifications | Consent |
| Google Gemini | USA | Offer text only | Consent |
| Payment | Ethiopia | Boost payments | Contract |
| CDN | Global | Static assets | No PII |

## 3. Safeguards
- Standard contractual clauses
- Data minimization before transfer
- No PII in AI prompts
- Encryption in transit

## 4. ECA Authorization
Sensitive categories require ECA pre-authorization.

## 5. Local Storage
Primary DB in Ethiopian data center (or compliant jurisdiction under ECA review).

## 6. User Rights
Users may object to specific transfers.

## 7. Review
Quarterly.
