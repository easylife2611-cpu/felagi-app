# Felagi Privacy Policy

**Effective:** 2026-10-01 | **Legal Basis:** Proclamation 1321/2024
**Controller:** Felagi | **Authority:** ECA

## 1. Data We Collect
Identity (Telegram ID, name), Profile (bio, region, locale), Marketplace (needs, offers, messages, ratings), Transaction (boost, payments), Technical (IP, user agent), Usage, Ad events (contextual only).

## 2. Lawful Basis (Art. 7-8)
| Purpose | Basis |
|---|---|
| Account/Service | Contract |
| Safety | Legitimate interest |
| Marketing | Consent |
| Legal compliance | Legal obligation |
| Sponsored ads | Legitimate interest |

## 3. Data Sharing
- Telegram: Auth, notifications
- Google Gemini: AI comparison (offer text only)
- Payment providers: Boost payments
- ECA: Regulatory on request

## 4. Cross-Border Transfer (Art. 28-30)
Data stays in Ethiopia where feasible. Transfers require consent, adequate protection, or ECA pre-authorization.

## 5. Data Subject Rights (Art. 33-40)
Information, access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, no automated decision.

## 6. Retention (Art. 18)
See DATA_RETENTION_POLICY.md

## 7. Security
TLS 1.3, encrypted at rest, RBAC, audit logging.

## 8. Breach Notification (Art. 30)
ECA + affected users within 72 hours.
