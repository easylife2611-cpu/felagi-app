# Data Subject Rights

**Legal Basis:** Proclamation 1321/2024, Art. 33-40
**Effective:** 2026-10-01

## 1. Eight Rights
| # | Right | Article | API |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Information | 33 | This doc |
| 2 | Access | 34 | GET /api/v1/privacy/data |
| 3 | Rectification | 35 | PATCH /api/v1/privacy/data |
| 4 | Erasure | 36 | DELETE /api/v1/privacy/data |
| 5 | Restriction | 37 | POST /api/v1/privacy/restrict |
| 6 | Portability | 38 | GET /api/v1/privacy/export |
| 7 | Objection | 39 | POST /api/v1/privacy/object |
| 8 | No automated decision | 40 | dpo@felagi.et |

## 2. Response Time
- Standard: 30 days
- Complex: +60 days with notice
- Urgent: 72 hours

## 3. Verification
Identity verification may be required.

## 4. Fees
Free, except repeated/vexatious requests.

## 5. Denial
Where law permits (legal hold), we explain.

## 6. Complaints
dpo@felagi.et or ECA.
