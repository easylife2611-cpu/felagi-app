# Data Protection Officer Appointment

**Legal Basis:** Proclamation 1321/2024, Art. 27
**Effective:** 2026-10-01

## 1. Requirement
Felagi processes personal data at scale, requiring a designated DPO.

## 2. Appointment
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | To be appointed |
| Email | dpo@felagi.et |
| Reports to | CEO / Board |
| Independence | No conflict of interest |

## 3. Duties
- Monitor compliance with Proclamation 1321/2024
- Advise on DPIAs
- Cooperate with ECA
- Contact point for data subjects
- Maintain RoPA

## 4. Authority
- Direct access to management
- Resources to perform duties
- No external instructions

## 5. Publication
Privacy Policy, App About, Website footer.

## 6. Review
Annually.
